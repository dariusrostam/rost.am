This paragraph exists to cluster several footnotes very close together on purpose,[^1] so that their natural reference points[^2] are only a line or two apart[^3] — close enough that, without collision handling,[^4] the notes in the margin would overlap each other[^5] instead of stacking cleanly with a small gap between them.[^6]

<Aside>This is an `<Aside>` — explicitly placed margin content rather than a footnote. It should take part in the same collision-avoidance stacking as the notes above and below it.</Aside>

Resize the window across roughly 1100px while this page is open: above that width the notes above should be stacked in the right margin without overlapping; below it they should collapse and only expand when their superscript number is tapped, with no jump to the bottom of the page.

One more paragraph, and one more note, further down — to confirm that notes recompute their position correctly after the dense cluster above, rather than permanently pushing everything below them down the page.[^7]

[^2]: Second — this is where collision handling starts to matter.
[^3]: Third. Still within the same sentence.
[^4]: Fourth. Their natural positions are nearly identical.
[^5]: Fifth. Each should be pushed down just enough to clear the one above it.
[^6]: Sixth and last of the cluster.
[^7]: A note well after the dense cluster, to confirm layout recovers to normal spacing afterward.
